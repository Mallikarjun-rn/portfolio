01 / ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Builder by choice.

I am an Electronics and Communication Engineering student with hands-on experience in Embedded Systems, PCB Design and IoT Development.

I enjoy turning ideas into working hardware — from writing firmware and interfacing sensors to designing circuit boards and testing complete systems.

I work with platforms such as ESP32, STM32, Arduino and Raspberry Pi, along with tools including Altium Designer, KiCad and STM32CubeIDE.

I also have practical exposure to SMT assembly, Pick-and-Place machines, PCB inspection, hardware testing, debugging and system integration.

My goal is to contribute to real-world products by building reliable and efficient embedded solutions.
